# AI Governance Case Studies

_Last Updated: May 2026_

This repository contains a growing collection of case studies examining AI governance, oversight models, and human–AI interaction in real-world contexts. Each study explores how AI systems behave in practice, how vendors articulate responsible AI commitments, and where operational mechanisms succeed—or fall short—in supporting those commitments.

This repository documents early-stage, observational research as I build foundational skills in AI governance analysis and human–AI interaction evaluation.

## Methods

These case studies reflect my perspective as an early‑career learner developing foundational skills in AI governance and human–AI interaction analysis. Each study is guided by a consistent, observational research approach:

### 1. Direct Observation  
Documenting system behavior, user interaction patterns, transparency practices, and visible safety or oversight mechanisms during demos or public sessions.

### 2. Cross‑Vendor Comparative Analysis  
Identifying shared themes, differences in governance maturity, and patterns in transparency, UX, and operational readiness.

### 3. Documentation and Claims Review  
Reviewing vendor statements, product descriptions, responsible AI commitments, and any publicly available technical or safety documentation.

### 4. Synthesis and Thematic Coding  
Organizing observations into structured themes to highlight governance gaps, transparency inconsistencies, UX risks, and implications for responsible deployment.

## Purpose

This repository serves as a reference point for understanding how AI systems are deployed, governed, and experienced by users in real-world contexts. The case studies included here explore:

- alignment (or misalignment) between policy and practice  
- operational realities of AI oversight  
- transparency and disclosure patterns  
- user experience and interaction risks  
- mechanisms required to support responsible AI at scale  

Together, these analyses contribute to an ongoing independent research effort focused on evaluating AI systems through the lenses of governance, oversight, and human–AI interaction.
