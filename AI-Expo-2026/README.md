# AI Governance Case Studies

This repository contains a growing collection of case studies examining AI governance, oversight models, and human–AI interaction in real-world contexts. Each study focuses on how AI systems behave in practice, how vendors articulate responsible AI commitments, and where operational mechanisms succeed—or fall short—in supporting those commitments.

## Case Studies

### AI Expo 2026
An in‑depth analysis of governance practices, oversight structures, and human–AI interaction patterns observed across multiple vendors at the 2026 AI Expo. This case study highlights gaps between stated responsible AI principles and the practical workflows required to implement them effectively.

## Purpose

The goal of this repository is to document and analyze how AI systems are deployed, governed, and experienced by users. These case studies serve as reference points for understanding:

- the alignment (or misalignment) between policy and practice  
- the operational realities of AI oversight  
- the role of human–AI interaction in system behavior  
- the mechanisms required to support responsible AI at scale  

This work reflects an independent research effort that bridges technical understanding with governance, user experience, and real-world system evaluation.
